About Me

A student with a love for technology

I'm Fatma Nasir, a 5th semester BS Computer Science student at Shaheed Benazir Bhutto University. My studies have taken me from the basics of programming to the core ideas behind modern software — how data is organised, how systems work, and how applications are designed and built.
